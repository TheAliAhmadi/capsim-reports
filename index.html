What’s inside each report

Every HTML report walks through the same coaching sections so you can scan quickly, then dig in where it helps most.

1

Financial Health

Cash, profit, inventory, and emergency loans — the “are we okay?” snapshot.

2

Investment Profile

Where the team spent: capacity, automation, TQM, and HR.

3

Segment Performance

For each market segment: your share and a plain-language why.

4

Distance from What Wins

How far each product is from what that segment rewards.

5

Customer Criteria

Price, age, MTBF, positioning, awareness, and accessibility.

6

Production & Inventory

Stockouts, leftover inventory, and production misses.

7

Competencies

R&D speed, costs, automation fit, margins, and capability signals.

8

Focus Going Forward

A short priority list for what to improve next.
