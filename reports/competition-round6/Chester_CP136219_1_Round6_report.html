Quick glossary (first-use Capsim terms): An emergency loan is automatic short-term borrowing Capsim forces when cash would otherwise go negative — expensive, and a signal that financing did not match spending. Contribution margin (CM) is the percent of each sales dollar left after variable production costs (material, labor, inventory carrying) — before SG&A, depreciation, and interest. Awareness is the share of customers who know your product exists (mainly promotion budget). Accessibility is how easy it is for customers to find and buy you in the sales channel (mainly sales budget, shared across products). A fine cut is the “sweet spot” on the perceptual map for a segment; customers strongly prefer products inside it. MTBF (Mean Time Between Failure) is reliability in hours; each segment has a required band. SG&A is selling, general & administrative expense (promotion, sales, admin). Net plant improvements on the cash flow statement are negative when you buy capacity/automation and positive when you sell capacity for cash. Plant utilization above 100% means a second shift: more output, but at higher labor cost.
